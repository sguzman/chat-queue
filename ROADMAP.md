## 🛡️ Non-negotiable gates

**G0: scope and security** — separate repository, no telemetry, strict extension allowlist, no public chat content.

**G1: browser-safe HUD** — installed in disposable Firefox Developer Edition, nonintrusive UI, all three themes, accessible editing and stable persistence.

**G2: trustworthy signals** — identity and epoch attribution, no stale restoration called fresh completion; fail closed on ambiguous state.

**G3: guarded send** — authenticated Firefox Developer Edition proves exact input, at most one send attempt, no draft overwrite, no wrong-chat dispatch and no blind retry.

**G4: external API** — real add-on-to-add-on Firefox tests, least-privilege read/append/control permissions, bounded payloads, revocation and recovery.

**G5: conditional engine** — deterministic bounded rules, no loops, user-authorized recovery and no unverified automatic retry.

**G6: release** — repeatable QA, Mozilla-compatible packaging/signing and public-artifact privacy audit.

Safety gates cannot be bypassed by a high percentage.
